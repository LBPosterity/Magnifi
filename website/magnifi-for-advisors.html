FAQ

Advisor questions, answered.

How does advisor pricing work?
Your advisor account is free, and so is building client files. A subscription only starts when you invite a client or share dashboards — $85/mo for the first entity in that client's group, $35/mo for each additional. No setup fees, no long contracts, month-to-month.
Do I have to use it on my own firm?
Not at all. Many advisors do, and find it helps them recommend from real experience — but it's entirely optional. You can go straight to building client files.
Do my clients need to be on Xero?
Xero is the smoothest path and we're an accredited Xero app, but client data can also be imported via Excel. Either way works.
Can I control what the client can see and do?
Completely. You decide whether the client has access at all, and what they can see if they do. Keep the file entirely in-house, or hand the day-to-day to the client — either way you oversee and manage the subscription.
Can I advise clients who aren't service businesses?
Magnifi is built around service businesses, but any client with employees benefits from the full forecast, labour cost modelling, multi-layer reporting and dashboards. The capacity methodology is most powerful for businesses that sell time — but the four-way forecasting and Profit to Cash features are useful across most industries.
How does the rolling cashflow work for my clients?
As actuals are imported from Xero, the forecast automatically updates from that point forward — so the file is never stale. When you walk into a client meeting, the numbers reflect where the business actually is, not where it was three months ago.
