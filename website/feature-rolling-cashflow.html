What it is

Your bank balance, twelve months forward.

A cashflow forecast that never updates when results come in isn't a forecast — it's a plan you've already drifted from. Magnifi solves this by treating the forecast as a rolling model: each import of actuals anchors the opening position to what actually happened, re-forecasts compliance obligations like GST and PAYGW from the new base, and applies your payment terms so receivables and payables land in cash at the right time.

The other part of "rolling" is that there's no fixed start date. You're not locked to the beginning of your financial year or the calendar year. Set the window to start from any month — useful if you want to see the next twelve months from today, model a mid-year scenario, or look ahead from the last actual period.

The result is a cashflow view that's always grounded in the current state of the business, starting exactly where you need it to start.

How it connects

Cashflow is the output of three connected models.

Cashflow isn't a standalone module in Magnifi — it's calculated from the same connected model that produces your P&L and balance sheet. Change anything upstream and the cashflow recalculates automatically.

P&L Forecast

Your income and expense forecast drives receivables and payables — the core of what flows through to cash. Payment terms are applied automatically so cash lands in the right month.

FAQ

Common questions

What does "rolling" actually mean?
Two things. First, the forecast re-forecasts forward each time you import actuals — the opening balance updates to what actually happened, compliance obligations recalculate from the new base, and the remaining months forecast forward from there. Second, there's no fixed start date. You're not locked to the beginning of your financial year or calendar year. You can set the start month to anything — useful if you want to see the next twelve months from today, or look ahead from your last actual period.
How does receivables timing work?
When you map your chart of accounts, you specify whether income or expense accounts flow through a receivables or payables account and set the payment terms. Magnifi then applies those terms when actuals come in — so if September income was $100,000 on 60-day terms, that $100,000 appears as cash collected in November, not September. This runs automatically every time you import.
Are GST, PAYGW, and Super included automatically?
Yes. GST, PAYGW, Payroll Tax, and Superannuation all appear in the cashflow under Taxes & Levies and run to the compliance terms set for each account. When actuals are imported, these re-forecast from the new base — so a stronger-than-expected income month flows through to a higher GST liability in the relevant settlement month.
Can I share the cashflow forecast with a client or board?
Yes. The cashflow chart is included in the live dashboard link you can share from any entity or group. The recipient sees the full dashboard — including the cashflow forecast — as at your most recent import. No login is required on their end, and you can revoke access at any time.
Do I have to be a service business to use Magnifi?
No. Magnifi is purpose-built for service businesses — professional services, consulting, trades, health and beauty, and similar people-driven operations — and the capacity planning features are designed specifically for this context. That said, any business that runs a connected P&L, balance sheet, and cashflow can use Magnifi's forecasting and reporting tools effectively.
