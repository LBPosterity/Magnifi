What it is

One forecast. Every level of your business.

Multi-level reporting in Magnifi is the ability to view your forecast and actuals at any layer of your organisation — consolidated group, individual entity, division, or custom segment — all from the same data, without rebuilding or exporting anything.

Most businesses outgrow a single P&L. As you expand — opening a new office, adding a service line, or defining teams around specific areas of delivery — the structure grows too, and staying across results at every level becomes harder. The numbers might look fine at the top until something goes wrong, and you need to know exactly which part of the business is driving it.

Magnifi structures your forecast to match how your business is actually organised. Set up the hierarchy once and every report — profit, cashflow, balance sheet, capacity — reflects it automatically. The group view consolidates; the entity view isolates; the division view goes deeper still.

How it works

Set up your structure once. Report at every level.

Magnifi mirrors how your business is actually organised. Here's how to get from a blank file to consolidated reporting.

1

Add your entities

Create a file for each legal entity. Connect each one to Xero or import via CSV — Magnifi's read-only Xero connection pulls in your chart of accounts and actuals automatically. Each entity gets its own P&L projection, cashflow forecast and balance sheet.

Add an organisation — Help Centre
2

Add divisions within each entity

If an entity has multiple offices, service lines or locations, add divisions. Assign income and cost accounts to the relevant division — shared overheads can remain unassigned and roll up at the entity level only. Employees can be split across divisions by percentage, with labour costs and capacity allocated accordingly.

Divisions — Help Centre
3

Group your entities for consolidated reporting

Create a Group and assign your entity files to it. Magnifi adds a third reporting layer: Group → Entity → Division. Mark any inter-entity accounts to exclude them from consolidated results. Group reports are available across Profit, Cashflow, Balance Sheet and Dashboards — and can be exported to Excel with a single click.

Groups & consolidation — Help Centre
4

Switch between levels instantly

Once your structure is set up, use the organisation selector to move between Group, entity and division views. The same forecast data underlies every level — there's no re-import, no reconciliation, no manual consolidation. A problem at the group level can be traced to an entity, then to a division, in seconds.

Reporting levels

Four levels of visibility. One source of truth.

Magnifi structures reporting into four layers. Each layer gives a different view of the same underlying forecast — so you always know what you're looking at and where to go next.

Level 01

Group

Consolidated results across all entity files in a group. Inter-entity transactions excluded automatically. The starting point for a board pack or management report.

e.g. XYZ Group — all three entities combined
Level 02

Entity

Results for a single legal entity — its own P&L, cashflow and balance sheet. Use this to isolate performance at the entity level before drilling further.

e.g. XYZ Financial Planning Pty Ltd
Level 03

Division

Results for one division within an entity — a branch, service line or location. Accounts and employees can be split across divisions with unassigned items rolling up to the entity view.

e.g. XYZ Financial Planning — NSW office
Level 04

Segment

A custom combination of divisions — report on any grouping that isn't captured by the standard hierarchy. Set up once; available alongside other reporting levels.

e.g. East Coast = NSW + QLD divisions

FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between a division and a segment?
Divisions are the core structural unit within a Magnifi file — a branch, service line, or location that has its own P&L projection with accounts and employees allocated to it. Segments are custom combinations of divisions that you create for a specific reporting view. For example, if you have NSW, QLD and VIC divisions, you could create an "East Coast" segment that combines NSW and QLD without changing the underlying division structure. Divisions are set up as part of your file structure; segments are layered on top for flexible reporting.
What's the difference between divisions and groups?
Divisions live inside a single Magnifi file and give you multiple reporting streams within one entity — or a simple consolidation if you set up each entity as a division. Groups connect separate entity files and add a third reporting layer above entities and divisions: Group → Entity → Division. Use divisions for simpler structures; use groups when each entity needs its own file and potentially its own divisions. See the organisation structure overview for guidance on which approach fits your situation.
How many entities or divisions can I have?
There's no hard limit on the number of entities. Magnifi charges per entity based on the number of connections, so you can add as many as you need. For divisions within a single file, we recommend keeping the number under 10 for optimal performance — but most businesses are well within that range.
How does Magnifi handle inter-entity transactions?
You mark accounts as inter-entity or inter-group in the Chart of Accounts. Inter-entity accounts are excluded when viewing at the All Divisions level; inter-group accounts are excluded when you export a group report to Excel. This keeps your consolidated figures clean without any manual adjustment each month.
Does multi-level reporting include cashflow and balance sheet, not just P&L?
Yes. All reporting levels — group, entity, division and segment — are available across Profit, Cashflow and Balance Sheet in Magnifi, as well as in Dashboards. The group consolidation also extends to the Dashboard charts. When you export a group report to Excel, it includes entity-level worksheets and a consolidated comparison across all reporting areas.
Do I have to be a multi-entity business to use Magnifi?
No. A single-entity, single-division file is the default and works perfectly well for businesses that don't need a more complex structure. Divisions and groups can be added at any time as the business grows — there's no need to restructure your file from scratch.
