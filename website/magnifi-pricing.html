FAQ

Pricing questions, answered.

Is there really a 30-day free trial?
Yes — for businesses, you get 30 days free with the full platform, no credit card required. Connect Xero, bring in your team and see your numbers before you pay anything.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. There are no long contracts — subscriptions are month to month, and you can cancel whenever you like.
How many users can I add?
Unlimited. You can invite your whole team — finance, operations, owners — at no extra cost. User seats are never a variable.
What does "fast, personalised support" mean?
It means you talk to people who know Magnifi deeply — not a chatbot or a tier-one help desk. Questions get real answers, quickly.
What counts as an entity?
Each Xero organisation (or imported file) is one entity. Within a single client group, the first entity is $85/mo and each additional entity is $35/mo. Two separate clients are billed separately.
How does advisor pricing work?
Your advisor account is free, and building client files is free. A subscription only starts when you invite a client or share dashboards. You can absorb the cost or pass it to the client, and you manage the subscription either way.
Are prices GST-inclusive?
No — all prices are quoted excluding GST. GST applies to Australian customers at the standard rate.
How do I pay?
By card, billed monthly. No setup fees and no minimum term.
Do I need to be on Xero?
Xero is the smoothest path — we're an accredited Xero app, so your data flows straight in. Not on Xero? You can import via Excel instead.
Do I have to be a service business?
No. Magnifi is built around service businesses, where capacity planning really shines. But any business with employees gets the full four-way forecast, employee-driven labour costs, multi-layer reporting and dashboards.
