FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to be a service business?
No. Magnifi is built around service businesses, where capacity planning really shines. But any business with employees gets the full four-way forecast, employee-driven labour costs, multi-layer reporting and dashboards.
Do I need to be on Xero?
Xero is the smoothest path — we're an accredited Xero app, so your data flows straight in. Not on Xero? You can import via Excel instead.
Will I need help to get started?
The guided onboarding walks you through each step. If you'd like a hand, implementation support from a chartered accountant is available — but most businesses are up and running on their own.
Can I model scenarios safely?
Yes. What-if lets you test changes instantly, and Playground gives you a sandbox copy so you can model in detail without touching your live file.
Is it built for a business my size?
Magnifi's sweet spot is service businesses in the 10–200 employee range — typically the point where an owner's gut feel for the numbers stops being enough and you need real financial control and clarity. It scales to multiple entities and divisions when you need it, and has been used by everything from small businesses to listed entities.
