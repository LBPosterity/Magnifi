The insight most businesses never see

Profit lives in the balance between your team and the work.

When capacity and income are in balance, profit is maximised. When you're small, gut feel keeps you on top of the numbers — but as you grow, it slips. More people, more moving parts, more ways for profit to quietly leak away. Magnifi makes the balance visible, so you can choose where you sit.

Too much capacity Idle time and wasted cost, eating into profit.
Too little capacity Burnt-out staff and customers you can't serve.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to be a service business?
No. Magnifi is built around service businesses, where capacity planning really shines. But any business with employees gets the full four-way forecast, employee-driven labour costs, multi-layer reporting and dashboards.
Do I need to be on Xero?
Xero is the smoothest path — we're an accredited Xero app, so your data flows straight in. Not on Xero? You can import via Excel instead.
How quickly can I get started?
Most businesses are looking at a real forecast within minutes — connect Xero, bring in your team, and the guided setup walks you through the rest.
Can I forecast multiple entities?
Yes. Add unlimited entities and divisions, and report at any level — group, entity or division — or combine divisions for segment reporting.
Is Magnifi new?
Not at all. We've been building and refining since 2017, and Magnifi has been used by everything from small businesses to listed entities.
